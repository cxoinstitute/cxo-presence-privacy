CxO Presence Heartbeat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

CxO Presence Heartbeat is an internal tool operated by CxO Institute and force-installed to managed staff Chrome profiles. This policy explains exactly what it collects and what it does not.

What it collects

For a managed @cxo-institute.com Chrome profile, and only while the browser is actively in use, the extension records:

  • the account email address of the signed-in profile, and
  • a 15-minute time block in which Chrome was active ("presence").
A single record contains only these two things, for example: person@cxo-institute.com was active during 09:15–09:30 on 2026-11-10.

What it never collects

The extension does not collect, read, store, or transmit:

  • URLs, web addresses, or domains you visit;
  • page titles or page content;
  • tabs, tab lists, or navigation history;
  • search queries, keystrokes, clicks, or form data;
  • anything about which websites or applications you use.

It requests no tabs permission and no access to page content, so it is technically incapable of observing your browsing.

How it is used

Presence blocks are aggregated into the organisation's internal HR activity dashboard to show working-hours activity for staff whose work is otherwise not visible in Google Workspace audit logs. It is used only for this internal workforce-reporting purpose.

Who can see it and where it is stored

Data is sent over HTTPS to a CxO-controlled endpoint and stored in a CxO Cloudflare database hosted in the West Europe region. Access is limited to CxO Institute IT and HR. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or any purpose unrelated to the internal activity dashboard.

Scope

Only managed @cxo-institute.com profiles report. Personal Google profiles and signed-out browsing are ignored entirely.
